PROJECT OVERVIEW

HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

ABOUT
  1. User enters slope geometry, soil properties and monitoring conditions.
  2. Engineering screening calculates Factor of Safety from displayed equations.
  3. Monitoring indicators are compared with disclosed prototype thresholds.
  4. Prototype AI-assisted risk engine combines normalized inputs with fixed weights.
  5. Risk level, warnings, contribution bars and engineer-review recommendations update.
  6. Trained ML models can replace the prototype after validated integration.
